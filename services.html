Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before booking your appointment.

Our gel manicures typically last 2–3 weeks without chipping. We use premium gel products and professional UV lamps for maximum durability. Longevity also depends on your nail health and daily activities — we recommend wearing gloves for heavy cleaning and avoiding prolonged water exposure.
Yes! We offer mobile nail services across major Nairobi neighbourhoods including Kilimani, Westlands, Karen, Lavington, and Parklands. A travel surcharge of KES 500–1,500 applies depending on your location. Select "Home Visit" when booking online and our team will confirm availability within 2 hours.
We accept M-Pesa (Till Number provided on booking confirmation), cash at the salon, and card payments via our POS terminal. For mobile visits, M-Pesa or cash only. You can also pay a deposit when booking online to secure your slot.
Absolutely! You're welcome to bring your favourite polish. We also stock over 200 colours in-salon, including premium gel shades and international brands such as OPI, CND Shellac, and Gelish. Our nail technicians are happy to help you pick the perfect shade for your skin tone.
We recommend booking at least 48 hours in advance, especially for weekends and bridal packages, as these slots fill up quickly. Walk-ins are welcome subject to availability. For same-day bookings, WhatsApp us directly at +254 700 000 000 for the fastest response.
We request 24 hours' notice for cancellations or rescheduling. Late cancellations may incur a 20% booking fee to cover the technician's reserved time. For bridal packages, a 50% deposit is non-refundable within 72 hours of the appointment.
